# Yatzy vs Bot

A free, browser-based Yatzy dice game where you play against a bot opponent.

## 🎲 About the Game

Yatzy is a classic dice game played with 5 dice and 13 scoring categories. You get 3 rolls per turn and must fill every category exactly once.

**Features:**
- Play against a bot with two difficulty levels (Optimal / Easy)
- Upper section bonus (63+ points = +50 bonus)
- Tracks your personal best score in your browser
- Works on mobile and desktop
- No download, no signup, no ads

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Roll the dice (up to 3 times per turn).
2. Click a die to hold it before rerolling.
3. After rolling, click a category on the scorecard to lock in your score.
4. Fill all 13 categories. Highest total wins.
